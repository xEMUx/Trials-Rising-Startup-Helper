Trials Rising Startup Helper


A lightweight Windows utility that helps launch **Trials Rising** on systems affected by startup issues.

The helper automatically closes Steam, starts Ubisoft Connect, launches the game, and repeatedly triggers Ubisoft Connect during startup to improve launch reliability.

  • -----NOTES-----
  • - Ubisoft Connect must already be signed in.
  • - Enable **"Remember me"** / automatic sign-in if available.
  • - Steam should be completely closed before launching the helper.
  • - The required trigger interval can vary depending on your **internet connection and system**.
  • - If the game does not start reliably, adjust the **Trigger Interval** and try again.
  • - Available interval: **0.5–3.0 seconds**.
  • --------------------

    • No Ubisoft credentials are stored or handled by the helper.
